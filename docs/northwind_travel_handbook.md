# Northwind Travel - Customer Handbook

(Fictional sample document for the demo.)

## Booking and payment

A booking is confirmed once a deposit of 20 percent of the trip price has been received. The remaining balance is due 45 days before departure. If the balance is not paid by that date, the booking is cancelled automatically and the deposit is not returned.

We accept bank transfer and credit card. Credit card payments carry a 1.5 percent processing fee. We do not accept cash or cheques.

## Cancellation and refunds

Cancellations made 60 days or more before departure receive a full refund minus the deposit. Cancellations made between 59 and 30 days before departure receive a 50 percent refund of the total trip price. Cancellations made fewer than 30 days before departure are not refunded.

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 14 business days of the cancellation being confirmed in writing.

If Northwind Travel cancels a trip, customers receive a full refund including the deposit, or may transfer to another departure at no charge.

## Group size and guides

Group trips run with a minimum of 8 and a maximum of 16 travellers. Every group is accompanied by one licensed guide. Groups of 13 or more travellers are accompanied by a second guide.

## Luggage

Each traveller may bring one suitcase of up to 20 kilograms and one piece of hand luggage of up to 8 kilograms. Extra luggage can be arranged for 35 euros per bag if requested at least 7 days before departure.

## Travel insurance

Travel insurance is mandatory on all trips outside the European Union. Customers must send proof of insurance at least 14 days before departure. Northwind Travel does not sell insurance directly but can recommend partners on request.

## Contact and support hours

The customer support team is available Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 Central European Time. During a trip, the emergency line is staffed 24 hours a day. The emergency line is only for travellers currently on a trip.
